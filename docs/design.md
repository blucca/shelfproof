# A proofing desk, with the page at its centre

Audience: freelance catalogue designers and small print studios checking an approved price list against final artwork. The core action is selecting a SKU row and seeing the exact price region on the proof.

## Visual plan

- Paper: #FFFFFF. Desk: #DDEAF2. Ink: #16324F. Proof blue: #185ADB. Mark red: #C1323D. Match green: #24704E.
- Type: Barlow Condensed for the compact brand and large lead line; Barlow for readable interface text and prices. The condensed face echoes retail price tickets. Sentence-case labels; tabular numerals in prices.
- Layout: a blue-grey proofing table, one large white artwork sheet at the left, a quiet price checklist at the right. A compact top tool strip carries the current files and export. On a phone, the chosen artwork region stays above its checklist row.
- Distinctive element: the actual proof page with a red correction circle and a blue line to the selected price row. The marks carry comparison state. Decorative dashboard cards and charts are excluded from this design.
- Entry: a two-column compact introduction, “Check the price on the page.” beside the real sample sheet, with “Try the sample proof” and “Use my files”. The proof desk is the main attraction.

## Review against the brief

A cream editorial palette was discarded: this task belongs to the cool light of a print studio's proof table. The prominent object is the user's page, keeping the visual hierarchy specific to artwork review. Condensed retail lettering supplies personality; standard interface text carries the work. Alignment is left-led, the central artwork is fitted to its natural ratio, and colours identify concrete review states.

## Interaction contract

A matched reading, a price mismatch, and an unresolved association have separate states. Every expected SKU stays visible. Clicking a row focuses its evidence region. A reviewer can select a price region and correct its transcription while retaining the original OCR reading. The exported report preserves both the machine reading and the reviewer decision.

## Extra-reading review (reader feedback, October 14 UTC)

Keep the proof table, Barlow type and existing six-colour palette. Put a full-width source strip directly below the artwork/checklist: “Unclaimed readings”, a count, then each OCR crop with its reading, kind and page. A blue rule ties this secondary check to the source-selection language; red remains reserved for established price differences. On phones the strip stacks into a single column. The same crops travel in the exported review.

Review: the new strip serves a specific print-production error: a leftover product or secondary price outside the current SKU selections. Source pixels remain the visual focus. The existing pairing desk and comparison rules stay in place.
